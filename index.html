Why four documents

Since 13 December 2024 the General Product Safety Regulation applies to every consumer product sold in the EU, including distance sales. Marketplaces now check listings for a named EU responsible person, traceability on the product, and warnings in the local language. These are the four artefacts that get checked.

1. Product label

Name and postal address of the EU responsible person, plus the manufacturer and a batch or serial number, on the product or its packaging.

2. Responsible person mandate

The written designation an EU company signs so it can act for a manufacturer outside the EU. Marketplaces and customs ask for it.

3. Declaration of conformity

Required for CE-marked categories: toys, electronics, batteries, and anything under a directive that names it.

4. Listing block

The exact text to paste into Amazon, Etsy or your own store so the responsible person and warnings appear before checkout.

Questions

Is this legal advice?

No. It is a template engine with the requirement list for each market, linked to the official registers. You or your EU partner remain responsible for the content.

Does it work for a seller outside the EU?

That is the case it is built for. A manufacturer outside the EU needs a responsible person established in the EU, and the label has to carry that person's name, postal address and email.

What about packaging and WEEE registration?

Those are separate per-country registers, listed on each card with the official link. This kit generates the documents, it does not register you.

Can I edit the output?

The pack prints from your browser, so text is selectable in the PDF. For layout changes, edit the JSON preset and reload.
